Who I am

Guglavath Sushanth Kumar

I’m a Product Manager with about five years in B2C and B2B SaaS products across mobile, TV and web. Most of that time has been in OTT streaming and FAST TV at YuppTV, with a stretch building a hyperlocal news platform from idea to MVP.

I start with how people actually use a product: analytics, session behaviour and interviews. Then I write the requirements engineering, design and QA need to build the right thing.

I’m most interested in content discovery, engagement and personalisation, and in the commercial side, including partnerships and billing workflows. I use ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini for research analysis and PRD drafting, and I’ve worked on integrating an AI/ML recommendation engine into OTT products.
